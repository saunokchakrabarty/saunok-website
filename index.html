Welcome! I am a PhD student in Economics at Purdue University. My research interests are in international trade and economic development. Specifically, I am interested in understanding the implications of trade policy changes in the US and the global economy, and the structure of markets and distortions in developing countries.

My job market paper tries to quantify caste-based misallocation in India and how it manifests within the distribution of firms and firm ownership. My second project focuses on characterizing product-level US trade policy in the early 20th century and its implications for the macroeconomic behavior of the US economy. My third project documents market fragmentation in India's agriculture sector and resulting implications for prices and welfare.
